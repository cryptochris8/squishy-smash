Magic Words (Codes)

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Magic words are Squishy Smash's codes: type one into the game's Magic Words panel and it grants free Sparkle Coins — and sometimes a keepsake. Here's the part other games can't say: codes in Squishy Smash never expire. Every word on this page will work today, next month, and next year. No countdowns, no "last chance" — that's a promise we make to kids, so it's a promise we make to you too.

How to redeem

  • Play Squishy Smash: The Lost Sparkle on Roblox (it's free)
  • Tap the Magic Words button
  • Type a word from this page and press Try it!

Hidden words

A few extra magic words are hiding out in the world: our picture books each hide a word that grants the exclusive Storybook Halo (you can't get it any other way — happy reading!), and our videos sometimes end with a word of their own. We don't list those here; finding them is the fun.

Why codes never expire here

Squishy Smash is built for younger kids, and our rule is simple: nothing in the game ever counts down, goes away, or punishes you for taking a day off. Sparkle Capsules are always free (earned with Sparkle Coins, never Robux), every friend stays earnable forever, and purchases are cosmetic only. Codes work the same way — once a word is real, it's real forever. Parents can read more on our For Parents section.
