# Squishy Smash — KDP Cover Brief + Title & Subtitle Options for Claude

## Purpose
This document helps Claude design the front cover direction and market positioning for the first Squishy Smash Amazon KDP character book.

This cover should:
- look premium
- feel collectible
- be highly visual
- work for Amazon KDP paperback
- attract parents and children quickly in thumbnail view
- match the interior character-book tone

This is for the first character/world introduction book, not the later storybook.

---

# 1. Core cover goal

The cover should instantly communicate:
- cute collectible characters
- a magical kid-friendly world
- premium polished art
- strong toy-like appeal
- a world children want to explore

The cover should make someone think:

**“This looks like a real character universe.”**

---

# 2. Cover type

## Recommended format
A children’s character/world book cover, not:
- a textbook cover
- a card binder cover
- a minimalist abstract cover
- a storybook conflict scene

This should feel like:
- a premium character introduction book
- a collectible universe book
- a giftable paperback

---

# 3. Best cover concept direction

## Recommended main concept
Use 3 to 5 standout characters from across the main packs on the cover.

This gives:
- variety
- instant visual richness
- clear worldbuilding
- stronger thumbnail appeal

## Best character mix
Claude should choose a balanced cover cast from:
- Squishy Foods
- Goo & Fidgets
- Creepy-Cute Creatures

### Recommended example mix
- Soft Dumpling or Galaxy Dumpling
- Goo Ball or Prism Stress Orb
- Blushy Bun Bunny or Mythic Plush Familiar
- optional bonus premium character for center emphasis

---

# 4. Best focal point strategy

Claude should choose one central hero focal character and support it with 2–4 surrounding characters.

That is better than:
- making all characters equal size
- overcrowding the cover
- creating a chaotic collage

## Best layout logic
- one largest hero in center
- smaller supporting cast around them
- magical world backdrop behind
- strong clear title at top
- subtitle below title or near lower middle
- enough breathing room so the art still reads clearly at Amazon thumbnail size

---

# 5. Visual style direction

Claude should preserve the Squishy Smash visual identity:
- glossy pastel finish
- rounded forms
- high-polish toy-like rendering
- soft magical sparkles
- bright but not messy
- premium, not cheap-looking

## Avoid
- flat generic vector look
- cluttered mobile UI elements
- too many tiny details
- dark muddy contrast
- tiny text
- card frame motifs that make it look like a pack of cards instead of a book

---

# 6. Best background direction

The background should suggest the wider world of Squishy Smash, not just float characters on blank color.

## Best background approach
Use a soft world-blend fantasy backdrop that combines hints of:
- dessert world
- goo world
- spooky-cute magical world

This can be done subtly with:
- clouds
- sparkles
- food shapes
- goo swirls
- creature silhouettes
- soft environment layering

The background should support the characters, not overpower them.

---

# 7. Title strategy

The main title should be:
- very readable
- bold
- child-friendly
- strong in thumbnail
- brand-first

## Best title structure
**Squishy Smash**
as the primary series title

Then a subtitle or book title extension that explains the book.

# 9. Best cover title recommendation

## Final recommendation
Claude should prioritize:

### Squishy Smash
### Meet the Squishies

Why:
- simple
- memorable
- easy for parents
- easy for kids
- thumbnail-friendly
- leaves room for later titles such as:
  - Squishy Smash: Squishy Foods
  - Squishy Smash: Goo & Fidgets

  - Squishy Smash Storybook Adventures

---

# 10. Typography guidance

Claude should use:
- large rounded playful title font
- strong contrast
- clean hierarchy
- minimal words on cover

## Recommended hierarchy
1. Squishy Smash
2. subtitle
3. optional author/publisher credit

## Avoid
- too many font styles
- tiny all-caps subtitle blocks
- thin fancy fonts
- low-contrast text over busy art

---

# 11. Subtitle placement

Best placement:
- directly under the main title
- or in a ribbon / glow panel below the title
- or slightly above lower third if title is huge

The subtitle should remain readable at thumbnail size.

---

# 12. Author / publisher name

Claude can keep author/publisher treatment small and elegant.

If shown:
- place near bottom
- do not overpower the title
- keep it subtle

---

# 13. Back cover direction

The back cover should be:
- clean
- magical
- readable
- not crowded

## Recommended back cover structure
- short book description
- small supporting character art
- enough blank space for barcode area
- soft background continuation from front cover

## Suggested back cover blurb direction
“Step into the bright, bouncy world of Squishy Smash and meet a cast of adorable characters from Squishy Foods, Goo & Fidgets, and Creepy-Cute Creatures. From sweet treats to spooky-cute friends, every squishy has a personality all its own.”

Claude can refine the blurb, but keep it short and child-friendly.

---

# 14. Spine strategy

Because the first book may be below the threshold for printed spine text or may have limited spine space, Claude should:
- keep spine planning simple
- not rely on detailed spine typography
- make the front cover strong enough on its own

If the final page count and KDP settings support spine text, use:
- Squishy Smash: Meet the Squishies

Otherwise, omit gracefully.

---

# 15. KDP-safe composition reminders

Claude should remember:
- important text must stay inside safe zones
- no essential details should sit near trim edges
- cover must eventually be built as a full wrap once final page count is locked
- front cover concept should still work even before final spine width is known

---

# 16. Thumbnail test rule

Claude should design the cover so it still reads when very small.

That means:
- large title
- clear hero characters
- limited clutter
- strong silhouette contrast
- no dependence on tiny decorative text

If it looks good only when zoomed in, it is not strong enough yet.

---

# 17. Recommended final cover concept for Claude

## Final concept directive
Create a premium, full-color children’s paperback front cover for **Squishy Smash: Meet the Squishies**.

Show:
- one large central hero squishy
- 2–4 supporting squishies from different packs
- a magical blended world backdrop
- glossy pastel collectible style
- highly readable title
- subtitle that clearly explains the book

The cover should feel:
- playful
- collectible
- magical
- polished
- giftable
- Amazon-friendly

Claude should design a cover that feels like the beginning of a real publishing franchise.
