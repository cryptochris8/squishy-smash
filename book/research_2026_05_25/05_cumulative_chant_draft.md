# Cumulative-Chant Manuscript Revision — Draft for User Review

*Drafted 2026-05-25. Proposes inserting a cumulative-chant device across Spreads 6, 8, 10 that breaks open in Spread 12's climax. Inspired by Wonky Donkey (Smith/Cowley) and Bear Snores On (Wilson/Chapman) — proven structures for read-aloud crescendos and TikTok-virality.*

**Mechanism:** Each of the three pack-worlds contributes its signature sound as the trio passes through it. The sounds **accumulate** across spreads — by Spread 10 the reader is saying all three. In Spread 12, the climax breaks the pattern: the three sounds fire **at once** as the EVERYBODY SQUISH shout-line lands. The cumulative chant earns the climax instead of declaring it.

**Pack sounds (lifted from existing canon, not invented):**
- Pudding Hills (Squishy Foods) → **Pmf** (Soft Dumpling's signature squish per STORY_BIBLE/build/config.py)
- Goo Coast (Goo & Fidgets) → **Sploink** (Goo Ball's signature squish)
- Moonlit Hollow (Creepy-Cute Creatures) → **Thup** (Blushy Bun Bunny's signature squish)

**Word count delta:** +51 words. New total ~928 (vs. 877 current). Still inside picture-book range; comfortable for ages 4–8 read-aloud.

**Voice-invariant compliance:**
- Periods, not em-dashes ✓

- No villain, no opposition added ✓
- Squishkeeper-style describing-by-feeling preserved ✓

## How this affects TikTok / read-aloud virality

The biggest commercial argument for the chant: **a video of a child performing the cumulative chant + the shout-line is short, kid-perfomable, and inherently shareable.** This is the Wonky Donkey pattern (granny + viral Facebook video → 2M+ copies sold). The current manuscript has only the shout-line as a kid-performable moment; the chant gives parents three more moments per read where the kid is doing something — and three more video-clip-able beats for the @CryptoChris8 giveaway promo content.
