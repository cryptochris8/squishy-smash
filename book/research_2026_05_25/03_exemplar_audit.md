# Squishy Smash: The Lost Sparkle — Picture Book Comp Deck

*Research output, 2026-05-25. 10 published exemplars from 2017–2025 that bracket the niche our book lives in: soft/kawaii-adjacent, ensemble-creature, bedtime-ritual-friendly, with strong commercial signal. Caldecott-aware where useful, leans toward what actually sells in the squishy/toy-tie-in adjacent market.*

---

## 1. Big — Vashti Harrison (2023)
- Little, Brown BFYR, ISBN 9780316353229. **2024 Caldecott Medal** (first Black woman); Coretta Scott King Honors; NYT bestseller.
- **Why it fits:** Soft round-bodied character work; tender emotional close; commercially scoped but Caldecott-aware — exactly our positioning.
- **Technique:** Digital + chalk pastel hybrid in Procreate. Soft warm palette (peach/cream/dusty pink). No hard outlines — forms built from shaded mass.
- **Composition:** Single-figure focus (rare for us). Physical gatefolds for the emotional pivot. Type sometimes typeset, sometimes laid over imagery as a hostile element.
- **Steal:** Soft chalk-pastel + digital is the single best technique reference for kawaii-adjacent ensemble that still reads as fine-art literary. Warm-cream palette = "Pudding Hills" instinct.
- **Avoid:** Gatefolds (too expensive for MVP). Don't copy the solo-protagonist rhythm.

## 2. Knight Owl — Christopher Denise (2022)
- Christy Ottaviano / Little, Brown, ISBN 9780316310628. **2023 Caldecott Honor;** #1 NYT bestseller; now a series.
- **Why it fits:** Most relevant single comp — cute creature + commercially huge + Caldecott honored. A small round owl that reads plushy. Cozy hero-with-heart energy maps onto our Squishies.
- **Technique:** Pencil under-drawing + Photoshop + Procreate. **Firelight + deep shadow rendering** — owl lit warm orange from below against navy/teal blue.
- **Composition:** Big chiaroscuro full-bleeds; cozy interior ↔ dramatic exterior threat. Text typeset, lives on bordered "tapestry" panels.
- **Steal:** **Light as a character.** Our "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" climax needs the same orange-against-blue chromatic punch. Also: small round protagonist can command dramatic spreads — don't shrink the canvas.
- **Avoid:** Medieval/dim palette; we want more saturation per kawaii brief.

## 3. Hot Dog — Doug Salati (2022)
- Knopf BFYR, ISBN 9780593308431. **2023 Caldecott Medal.**
- **Why it fits:** Bedtime-adjacent emotional regulation arc. Sibling to our "Every pop is a hello. Every hello comes back."
- **Technique:** Mixed digital with hand-drawn line; loose painterly washes. Limited palette per setting — hot orange/yellow city, cooling blue/teal beach.
- **Composition:** **Pacing-by-panel-density.** Chaotic urban opens with dense gridded panels (overwhelm visualized); seaside explodes into single wide horizontals (relief).
- **Steal:** **Panel density as emotional volume.** Our 3-world structure can use this — chaotic pre-climax with multiple panels, closing Moonlit Hollow breathes with single horizontals.
- **Avoid:** Hot Dog is solo + emotionally austere; we have a shout-line.

## 4. The Bad Seed / The Good Egg / Couch Potato — Jory John & Pete Oswald (2017–2020)
- HarperCollins. The Food Group series. **Multiple NYT #1/#2 bestsellers.**
- **Why it fits:** **The** commercial benchmark for cute anthropomorphic ensemble in a high-concept world. Squishy Smash is doing for squishy creatures what Food Group does for groceries.
- **Technique:** Watercolor + digital finish. Soft inked outline. Bright saturated primary palette. Backgrounds often flat color washes — *characters* carry detail.
- **Composition:** Character-on-flat-color spreads alternate with busier "world" spreads. **Hand-lettered shout balloons** for emphasis. Heavy face-beat close-ups.
- **Steal:** **Speech bubbles for shout-line moments.** "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" wants to be a hand-lettered shout balloon. Reduce backgrounds, amplify character.
- **Avoid:** Cartoon-comic outline weight too much for our Caldecott-aware register.

## 5. Pokko and the Drum — Matthew Forsythe (2019)
- Paula Wiseman / Simon & Schuster, ISBN 9781481480390. Boston Globe-Horn Book Honor; PW Best Book.
- **Why it fits:** Frog protagonist + emerald-forest ensemble that grows across the book. Quiet-to-loud arc culminates in a parade — direct structural sibling to our climax.
- **Technique:** Watercolor + gouache + colored pencil. Saturated mossy green; warm sunlight. Almost screenprint-feeling.
- **Composition:** **Slow accretion.** First spreads nearly empty save Pokko. Each spread adds an animal. Final parade is horizontal full-bleed packed with cumulative cast.
- **Steal:** **Accretive ensemble climax.** Our shout-line spread should be visually fullest, every Squishy cumulated. Build to it by adding one squish per page-turn.
- **Avoid:** Forest-mossy palette too dim for kawaii brief.

## 6. Bear Snores On — Karma Wilson & Jane Chapman (2002, series ongoing)
- Margaret K. McElderry / S&S, ISBN 9780689831874. NYT bestseller; 12+ book series.
- **Why it fits:** **The** archetype for bedtime call-and-response with a creature ensemble accreting in one cozy space. "But the bear snores on" is exactly the refrain spine our book needs.
- **Technique:** Acrylic painterly. Warm cave-firelight palette. Soft fur — every animal feels plush.
- **Composition:** Refrain-driven. Each new animal entry gets a full-bleed cozy interior.
- **Steal:** **The refrain structure.** Pattern: three pack-worlds, each ending near-identical refrain, then climax breaks the pattern.
- **Avoid:** Chapman's painterly traditionalism reads 2002, not 2026.

## 7. Press Here — Hervé Tullet (2011, perpetual frontlist)
- Chronicle Books, ISBN 9780811879545. **Longest-running picture book on NYT bestseller list.** 5M+ copies sold.
- **Why it fits:** Interactive shout-line / instruction-driven engagement is structurally the same move as "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" Tullet pioneered the modern grammar.
- **Technique:** Flat gouache dots on raw white. Zero rendering.
- **Composition:** Hand-lettered instructions; massive negative space; **page-turn itself is the gameplay.**
- **Steal:** **Page-turn-as-reveal.** Spread before "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" should ask the reader to shout/press/squish; climax spread is the consequence.
- **Avoid:** Minimalism opposite of our richly-rendered world.

## 8. Don't Push the Button! — Bill Cotter (2013, perpetual frontlist)
- Sourcebooks Jabberwocky, ISBN 9781402287466. **1M+ copies across series.** 15+ languages.
- **Why it fits:** Larry the purple monster + interactive shout-line + simple kawaii design. **The** commercial proof that a single cute monster + shout-line can sell 1M+. Closer to our commercial register than Caldecott books.
- **Technique:** Flat digital vector. Bold inked outlines. Saturated purple/yellow/red. Larry = round eyes + round body — minimum-viable plushy monster.
- **Composition:** Single-character on flat color spreads. Type huge, hand-lettered, often *is* the composition.
- **Steal:** **Character-on-flat-color spreads work commercially.** When tempted to over-render Goo Coast or Pudding Hills, remember Cotter sold 1M with monochrome backgrounds. 2–3 of our 18 spreads should be character-on-flat-color.
- **Avoid:** Bold-outline cartoon feels too cheap for Caldecott-aware register.

## 9. The Wonky Donkey — Craig Smith & Katz Cowley (2009, viral 2018)
- Scholastic. **2M+ copies in print after Scottish grandmother's viral Facebook read-aloud.**
- **Why it fits:** **The** modern proof that goofy read-aloud picture book can go viral via single performance video. Squishy Smash is designed for TikTok read-alouds via giveaway campaign — this is the exemplar.
- **Composition:** **Cumulative tale.** Each spread adds an adjective. Page-turn payoff is the **accumulating chant.**
- **Steal:** **Accumulating chant.** "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" wants to be earned by a chant building across the three worlds — Pudding Hills word + Goo Coast word + Moonlit Hollow word, then SHOUT together. Most likely to go viral on TikTok read-aloud.
- **Avoid:** Wobbly traditional watercolor dated.

## 10. Little Elliot, Big City — Mike Curato (2014, 5-book series)
- Henry Holt / Macmillan, ISBN 9781627796989. 12 languages.
- **Why it fits:** Pastel polka-dotted elephant — **literally the most squishmallow-adjacent character design in mainstream literary picture books.** Elliot is plushy, round, pastel, IP-ready toy. Curato proves commercial-meets-literary balance.
- **Technique:** Graphite pencil + digital color. Soft chalk-feeling pastels with moody 1930s-NYC cityscapes around Elliot.
- **Composition:** **Tiny-in-a-big-world.** Elliot always *small* in frame — the world towers. Alternates intimate close-ups + wide environmental shots.
- **Steal:** Each of our three pack-worlds should have at least one "tiny Squishy in vast world" establishing spread. This is what makes a kawaii character feel like they live in an *actual place.*
- **Avoid:** Don't borrow Elliot's loneliness — borrow his scale rhythm.

# Synthesis — Squishy Smash's 7 specific moves

1. **Build to "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" with a cumulative chant.** Wonky Donkey + Bear Snores On + Pokko prove the structure. Three pack-worlds each contribute one chant-word; climax breaks pattern. **Highest TikTok-virality leverage.**

2. **Use the page-turn before the shout-line as Press Here / Don't Push the Button setup-and-reveal.** Spread 11 asks the reader to shout. Spread 12 is the consequence (every Squishy mid-launch, full-bleed, max chromatic punch — orange-against-blue per Knight Owl).

3. **Chalk-pastel + digital is the right medium register.** Big (Vashti Harrison) is the closest technique reference. Soft warm palette, no hard outlines, forms built from shaded mass. Differentiates from Bad Seed/Don't Push the Button cartoon-outline competitors and earns Caldecott-aware perception. Skip gatefolds.

4. **Vary panel density as emotional volume per Hot Dog.** Pre-climax dense/gridded (gathering chaos); bedtime close single horizontals that breathe. "Every pop is a hello. Every hello comes back." wants the maximum-quiet spread.

5. **At least 2–3 spreads should be character-on-flat-color (Don't Push the Button / Bad Seed grammar).** Retail eye-catch and shout-line punch depend on a few simplified spreads. Also speeds production.

6. **Tiny-character-in-vast-world establishing shot per Little Elliot for each pack-world.** Spread 6 (Pudding Hills wide), Spread 8 (Goo Coast wide), Spread 10 (Moonlit Hollow wide) — each shows a single Squishy small in frame so the world feels real before close-ups.

7. **Speech-bubble the shout-line; typeset the narration.** Per Bad Seed: hand-lettered shout balloons for emphasis ("EVERYBODY SQUISH!"), clean typeset for narrative + bedtime close. Visual register shift telegraphs the read-aloud performance to the adult reader.

**Avoid:** Don't try to be Caldecott-pure (Watercress / Hello Lighthouse register) — our commercial scope is closer to Knight Owl + Big + Little Elliot, which are Caldecott-respected but mass-market. Don't render every spread with same density. Don't be afraid of hand-lettering at the climax. Don't make the bedtime close talky — Salati and Klassen prove quietest spread is most resonant.
