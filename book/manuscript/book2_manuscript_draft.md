## Spread 1 — Three Places at Peace

> Three places had wobbled themselves into being, one for each first feeling. Pudding Hills, where comfort spilled warm. Goo Coast, where surprise spilled glossy. Moonlit Hollow, where the brave-cuddle spilled quiet. Above them all, the Sparkle held. Small and bright. The way being seen is always bright.

**Visual direction:** Wide establishing shot showing all three pack-worlds at peace with the Sparkle visible above. For production this needs a net-new fourth plate (the three-region overview); for POC, the Pudding Hills plate with the Sparkle painted in is acceptable.

## Spread 2 — The Flicker

> And then. The Sparkle did something it had never done. It flickered. It wobbled. It split, very gently, into three. One shard drifted toward Pudding Hills. One drifted toward Goo Coast. One drifted toward Moonlit Hollow. The three places, for the first time, felt a little quieter.

**Visual direction:** Sky-focused composition. The Sparkle visibly cracking into three; three glowing shards trailing toward each pack-world's silhouette on the horizon. No characters yet. Most painterly of the early spreads.
**Word count:** 53
**Voice notes:** "And then." as a one-beat opener — bible Voice (varying sentence shapes, periods not em-dashes). The catastrophe is described with calm — the stakes land via *"felt a little quieter,"* not via alarm. Sparkle planted in Spread 1 pays off here.

## Spread 3 — Three Look Up

> In Pudding Hills, Soft Dumpling looked up. In Goo Coast, Goo Ball looked up. In Moonlit Hollow, Blushy Bun Bunny looked up. None of them had ever left home before. But each of them, very quietly, decided it was time.

**Visual direction:** Triptych — three vertical panels side by side, one per protagonist standing at the edge of their pack-world looking up toward the dimming sky. Uses **scared** pose variants (the moment-before-bravery look). Each panel's background is a slice of that pack's plate.
**Word count:** 47
**Voice notes:** Parallel triple-structure introduces all three names cleanly. "But each of them, very quietly, decided it was time" — the closeness-as-courage thesis (`STORY_BIBLE.md` §5) lands without explaining itself.

## Spread 4 — At the Border

> Soft Dumpling reached the edge of the Pudding Hills and stopped. The grass was different here. Greener. Glossier. Just past where the syrup river thinned to a trickle, somebody glossy was waiting. *Sploink,* said Goo Ball, by way of hello. Soft Dumpling tilted. *Pmf,* she answered, which is dumpling for *hello back.*

**Visual direction:** Border-line composition — left half Pudding Hills (peach/cream), right half Goo Coast (mint/aqua). Soft Dumpling on the left edge (neutral pose), Goo Ball on the right (neutral pose). The syrup river thinning into the goo tide is the visual gag at the bottom of the spread.
**Word count:** 56
**Voice notes:** Per the test beat — italic on signature squishes (*Sploink*, *Pmf*) and on the Squishkeeper's gloss (*hello back*). Periods, not em-dashes. "Somebody glossy was waiting" is the Squishkeeper-voice trick of describing a character by their feeling, not their identity.

## Spread 5 — Three

> A third sound came from behind them. *Thup, thup.* Blushy Bun Bunny hopped out from somewhere between the two worlds, cheeks rosier than usual. "Oh good," said the bunny. "I had a feeling you would find each other." And the three of them, three different feelings standing on a boundary nobody had ever crossed, very gently started walking.

**Visual direction:** All three protagonists together on the border seam — neutral poses, mid-stride to the right. The Pudding Hills/Goo Coast border continues but a hint of Moonlit Hollow's lavender appears at the far horizon (foreshadowing the third act). Walking-toward-camera-right composition implies the journey has begun.
**Word count:** 61
**Voice notes:** Blushy Bun Bunny is the only protagonist who speaks dialogue in the opening five spreads — establishes her as the trio's gentle leader-by-warmth. The closing image ("three different feelings standing on a boundary nobody had ever crossed") is the test beat's load-bearing line, validated for voice. "Very gently started walking" is the soft-motion close that builds toward the cozy ending model.
