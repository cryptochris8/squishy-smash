## The Beat — ~185 words

Three packs had stood apart since the very first wobble. Three regions, three feelings, three worlds. Squishies in the Pudding Hills had never met a squishy from the Goo Coast. The Goo Coast had never met Moonlit Hollow. It was simply how things were.

Until the day the Sparkle started to dim, and three small squishies were brave enough to leave home.

Soft Dumpling reached the edge of the Pudding Hills and stopped. The grass was different here. Greener. Glossier. Just past where the syrup river thinned to a trickle, somebody glossy was waiting.

"Sploink," said Goo Ball, by way of hello.

Soft Dumpling tilted. *Pmf*, she answered, which is dumpling for *hello back*.

A third sound came from behind them — *thup, thup* — and Blushy Bun Bunny hopped out from somewhere between the two worlds, cheeks rosier than usual.

"Oh good," said the bunny. "I had a feeling you would find each other."

And the three of them, three different feelings standing on a boundary nobody had ever crossed, very gently started walking.

I went with them, of course.

## What this beat reveals

**Strengths:**
- The Squishkeeper-narrator voice survives a multi-character scene without confusion about whose POV we're in
- The cross-pack premise lands in 30 words ("Pudding Hills had never met a squishy from the Goo Coast")
- Character voice is differentiated by *sound*, not adjectives — Goo Ball is glossy because he says *sploink*, Soft Dumpling is warm because she says *pmf*. This scales to all 48.
- The closing line works as both a Book 2 echo and a brand sign-off ("I went with them, of course" could appear on the back cover, a sticker, the App Store, anywhere — same energy as the Pact line)

**Risks / things to watch in full manuscript:**
- The Squishkeeper-first-person voice on the close means every chapter needs a similar Squishkeeper hand-off line. Build a small library of these so they don't feel formulaic across 18 spreads.
- "Three different feelings standing on a boundary" is on the edge of abstract for a 4-year-old. Acceptable here because it sits at the end of a concrete scene; would be too much in isolation. Calibrate per spread.
- The Pact line ("Every pop is a hello. Every hello comes back.") didn't naturally fit this beat. That's fine — save it for the climax beat (the moment the Sparkle returns).

**Verdict: Voice validated.** The bible holds up under prose. The discovery-spike Step 1 gate passes. Recommend proceeding to Step 2 (Amazon competitive-set scan) and Step 3 (parent-problem articulation) per `STORYBOOK_DISCOVERY.md` §8.
