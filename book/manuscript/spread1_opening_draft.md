## The prose (52 words)

> Three places had wobbled themselves into being, one for each first feeling. Pudding Hills, where comfort spilled warm. Goo Coast, where surprise spilled glossy. Moonlit Hollow, where the brave-cuddle spilled quiet. Above them all, the Sparkle held. Small and bright. The way being seen is always bright.

## What this opener does for the rest of the book

- **Names all three packs in 30 words** so the reader has a mental map before the trio is introduced.
- **Establishes "first feeling"** as the framing the rest of the book leans on (each Common protagonist is the embodiment of their pack's first feeling).
- **Plants "the Sparkle held"** so when it flickers next spread, the loss is felt without exposition.
- **Final sentence sets the book's emotional thesis** — being seen is the source of light. Echoes the bible's one-sentence world law.

## Open editorial questions for the user

- **"Brave-cuddle"** — the bible's canonical term for Moonlit Hollow's first feeling. Reads slightly clunky in prose; could substitute "the soft dark" if you prefer. (The bible word stays canon either way; this is a prose-level call.)
- **"Above them all"** vs. a more concrete placement — could swap to "Between them all" if the visual shows the Sparkle as a shared light bridging the three packs (which the spine implies).
