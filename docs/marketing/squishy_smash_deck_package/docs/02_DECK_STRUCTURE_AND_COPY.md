# Deck Structure and Draft Copy

Create approximately 12 to 15 slides. Keep copy concise and highly visual.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

### Title
Squishy Smash

### Subtitle
Licensing & Manufacturing Opportunity

### Supporting Line
An original character universe ready to become a physical collectible toy line.

### Visual Direction
Feature the Squishy Smash logo and a collage of 3 to 5 hero characters. Use a polished, vibrant background with subtle squishy textures.

## Slide 2 — The Opportunity

### Heading
From Digital Characters to Real-World Collectibles

### Copy
Squishy Smash is a fully developed children's character brand with original art, books, games, trading cards, and 3D character models already in place.

The next step is bringing selected characters into the physical world as collectible slow-rise squishy toys.

### Key Callout
The brand foundation is built. Product development can begin with existing character assets.

## Slide 5 — Manufacturing Readiness

### Heading
Built for a Faster Path to Prototype

### Copy
Existing 3D character models can be adapted for manufacturing, reducing the need to begin from a blank concept.

### Process Graphic
Existing Model → Manufacturing Review → Mold-Ready Geometry → Prototype → Production Sample

### Supporting Points
- OBJ, FBX, or STL files can be supplied
- High-resolution character references are available
- Color and front/side/back views can be provided
- Minor design-for-manufacturing adjustments are expected

## Slide 6 — Proposed Product

### Heading
Collectible Slow-Rise Squishy Characters

### Product Concept
- Soft polyurethane foam construction
- Slow-rise squeeze response
- Approximately 4 to 6 inches tall
- Durable painted finish
- Optional scent, glitter, glow, or color-change variants
- Individual retail packaging or blind-box format

### Note
Do not lock final specifications. Present these as initial target specifications for manufacturer discussion.

## Slide 7 — Pilot Collection

### Heading
Recommended First Release

### Name
Squishy Smash Founders Series

### Initial Approach
Launch with 4 to 6 hero characters instead of all 48.

### Benefits
- Lower tooling investment
- Faster testing
- Clearer customer feedback
- Easier quality control
- Creates anticipation for future series

### Visual Direction
Show 4 to 6 large character images as a proposed launch lineup. Clearly label them as proposed selections unless final names are provided.
