# Manufacturer RFQ Content

This content may be used as a final slide appendix or as a separate one-page document.

## Project
Custom slow-rise polyurethane foam squishy based on an original Squishy Smash character.

## Available Inputs
- Existing 3D model
- Front, side, and back visual references
- Character color artwork
- Logo and branding assets
- Packaging concept

## Initial Target Specifications
- Size: approximately 4 to 6 inches
- Material: slow-rise polyurethane foam
- Finish: durable painted coating
- Packaging: individual box, bag, or blind box
- Quantity: prototype first, then pilot production

## Information Requested
1. Prototype and sample cost
2. Mold and tooling cost per character
3. Minimum order quantity
4. Unit cost at 250, 500, 1,000, and 5,000 pieces
5. Estimated development timeline
6. Estimated production timeline
7. Available foam densities and rebound speeds
8. Paint and coating options
9. Scent, glitter, glow, or color-change options
10. Packaging capabilities
11. Product safety testing support
12. Shipping terms and estimated freight options
13. Ownership and storage of molds
14. Revision policy for samples
15. Payment schedule

## Safety and Compliance Questions
Ask whether the manufacturer can support applicable U.S. toy-safety requirements, including age grading, labeling, tracking information, and third-party testing where required.

Do not treat this list as legal advice. Confirm requirements with qualified compliance professionals and testing laboratories before sale.
