## What to do after submitting

1. **Save a copy** of your submission. Google Forms doesn't always email confirmation; screenshot the "thanks for submitting" page.
2. **Wait 7 business days.** Hub City is responsive but not instant. Don't follow up before day 8.
3. **Day 8 follow-up email** (if no response) — send to **info@hubcity.org**:
   ```
   Subject: Quick follow-up — Squishy Storytime event request (Campbell)
   
   Hi Hub City team,
   
   I submitted an event request via the bookshop form last week for a Saturday morning Squishy Storytime built around my new children's picture book series. Just bumping to make sure it landed in the right inbox — happy to provide any additional info or drop a copy by the shop for the team to take a look.
   
   Thanks!
   Chris Campbell
   chriscam8@gmail.com
   ```
4. **If you get a yes**: ask about the Hub Writers Project Membership signup link and event date options. Send the sell sheet PDF as soon as you have a date.
5. **If you get a no**: pivot to consignment-only ask — "I understand the event calendar is tight; would Hub City be open to consigning 5 copies of each title on shelf?" Hub City does consign indie titles even when events aren't a fit.
