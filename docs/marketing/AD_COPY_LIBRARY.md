## 2. Meta (Instagram + Facebook)

*Format: **Primary text** (first ~125 characters carry the ad — everything after "See more" is a bonus) · **Headline** (≤40 chars) · **Description** (≤30 chars).*

### 2.1 Gift angle — "Grandma" audience

**A1**
- **Primary:** Picking a book for a 5-year-old is a gamble. This is the one they hand back and ask you to read again. Three little squishies from three different worlds set out to find each other — a full read-aloud adventure with a soft, quiet landing at the end. Ages 4–8. 40 pages, full color.
- **Headline:** `The one they'll ask for again`
- **Description:** `Picture book, ages 4–8`

**A2**
- **Primary:** If there's a 4-to-8-year-old on your list who loves squishy, soft, cute things — this is the book version of that feeling. Gentle story, no scary parts, and an ending made for bedtime.
- **Headline:** `A gift for the squishy-obsessed`
- **Description:** `Ages 4–8 · full color`

**A3 (peak season, Nov 25 – Dec 13)**
- **Primary:** Still need one more thing under the tree for a little one? A soft, full-color picture book about three friends finding each other. Ages 4–8, and it arrives before Christmas.
- **Headline:** `Arrives before Christmas`
- **Description:** `Ages 4–8 picture book`

### 2.2 Bedtime & safety — "Mom of a 4–8" audience

**B1**
- **Primary:** No villains. No scary parts. No loud lesson at the end. Just three small friends being brave together, and a last page that lands softly enough to actually put a kid to sleep. Ages 4–8.
- **Headline:** `A bedtime book that lands soft`
- **Description:** `No villains. No scary parts.`

**B2**
- **Primary:** The rhythm a read-aloud is supposed to have: it gets bigger, then quieter, then bigger again — and then it puts them down gently. 18 full-color spreads, ages 4–8.
- **Headline:** `Built to be read out loud`
- **Description:** `18 full-color spreads`

**B3**
- **Primary:** Your kid gets one line to shout in the middle of this book. "EVERYBODY SQUISH!" They will find it every single time. Ages 4–8.
- **Headline:** `They get a line to yell`
- **Description:** `Read-aloud picture book`

### 2.3 Squishy aesthetic

**C1**
- **Primary:** If your kid loves squishies, this is that feeling in a book. Soft, round, glowing, and full of small friends who like each other. Ages 4–8.
- **Headline:** `For kids who love squishies`
- **Description:** `Soft. Round. Glowing.`

### 2.4 Dad story — the share engine (organic first, boost the winner)

**D1**
- **Primary:** I have three daughters. I wanted them to have a world that was soft — no villains, nothing scary, everybody nice to each other. So I wrote them one, and then I put all three of them in it as characters. This is book two. They picked which spreads stayed.
- **Headline:** `I wrote it for my three girls`
- **Description:** `Book 2 of the series`

**D2**
- **Primary:** My twin 8-year-olds were the first focus group, and they would not lie to me about whether a page was working. If a spread made them go quiet, that was the only data I needed.
- **Headline:** `The focus group was my kids`
- **Description:** `A picture book, ages 4–8`

### 2.5 Series / collector

**E1**
- **Primary:** Book 1 is the field guide — 48 squishy friends, one per page, the kind of book a kid reads with a finger on the page. Book 2 is their first real story. They work either way, but together they're better.
- **Headline:** `Start with one, get both`
- **Description:** `Books 1 & 2, ages 4–8`
