# Promo Video Shot List + Scripts

Shoot-ready companion to `tiktok_campaign.md`. The campaign doc holds
the strategy (pillars, hooks, posting plan, hashtags); this holds the
concrete clips to capture against the **1.2 build**.

Note: 1.2's new bits (round timer, menu music) aren't viral hooks —
these clips lean on the evergreen core (satisfying pops + the mythic
reveal), shot on the juicier 1.2 bursts (P2-C).

---

## Shot list — footage to capture

Record vertical **9:16**, full screen brightness, game audio on.

| # | Footage | Notes |
|---|---------|-------|
| A | Mythic reveal ×3-4 takes | Skybox crossfade + shake + gold burst. The money shot. |
| B | Sustained pop chains | 10-15s of rapid popping, combo counter climbing. |
| C | One slow, dramatic single burst | Build pressure, let one big pop land with goo splatter. |
| D | A rare/epic burst | Shows off the bigger P2-C tier-tinted burst. |
| E | Collection album | Scroll the card grid; a mythic card sitting unlocked. |

Mythics are rare by design — to capture clean shot-A takes, temporarily
raise the mythic spawn rate on the dev build, then revert it.
